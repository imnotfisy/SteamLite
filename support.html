Quick fixes

SteamLite reads your library through Steam. Check that your Steam profile's game details are set to public, that the Steam ID is right, and that your Steam Web API key is valid. Then press refresh in the Library.

It is free. Get one from Steam's website while signed in to your Steam account, then paste it into SteamLite when you sign in. The key is stored on your PC.

Download the installer for the version you want from the download section or the releases page and run it. It installs over your existing SteamLite and keeps your settings.

Drops, the shop, the trophy room, events, wishlist and sales, inventory and the tools hub are not in 10.1.5 yet. If you need them, download 9.4.1 from the releases page and skip the update. See the changelog for details.

Check the server status at the top of this page. If it says unreachable, the SteamLite Online server is down or your network is blocking it; try again in a few minutes.

Make sure the PC app and the phone app are both signed in to the same Steam account and online. Profile changes can take a couple of minutes to appear on the other device.
