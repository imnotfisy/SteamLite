Quick fixes

Set Game details to Public in your Steam privacy settings, then press refresh in the library. If it still shows nothing, sign out and sign in with Steam again.

Finish signing in on the Steam page that opened in your browser. If you closed it, press Open the page again in SteamLite. If SteamLite says it could not reach the sign-in server, check your internet connection and try again.

It is free. Get one from Steam's website while signed in to your Steam account, then paste it into SteamLite when it asks. It is saved to your account.

Download the installer for the version you want from the download section and run it over your current SteamLite. Your settings and play time are kept.

SteamLite counts a game from when it is really running. Closing SteamLite while a game runs stops the counting, so keep it open or leave it in the tray. Games that start through a launcher are followed while anything runs from the game's folder.

Messages and friends, themes, the in-game overlay, streaks, the rewards system, cloud backup and Discord presence are not in 26.11. If you rely on them, 10.1.5 is still available.
