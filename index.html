Getting started

Ready in about a minute.

Download

Get the installer and press Install. It installs just for you, so it normally needs no administrator rights.

Sign in with Steam

Steam's own sign-in page opens in your browser. Make sure your profile's game details are public.

Press Play

Your library loads. Pick a game and press Play. SteamLite counts the time.

Questions

Short answers.

SteamLite opens Steam's own sign-in page in your browser, so it never sees your password. The sign-in is confirmed through the SteamLite server, and the token it saves is protected by Windows.

Steam needs a free Web API key to hand over your game list. SteamLite asks once and saves it to your account. You can get one from Steam's website.

Set Game details to Public in your Steam privacy settings, then press refresh in the library.

Yes. Install 26.11 over 10.x. Your settings, favorites, collections and play time are read from the same place, and you sign in with Steam once.

To keep SteamLite focused, these are not in 26.11: Messages and friends, themes, the in-game overlay, streaks, the rewards system, cloud backup and Discord presence. If you rely on them, 10.1.5 is still available.

Stable if you want finished updates only. Beta if you want new features before the stable release gets them. The release notes for every version are on the changelog.
