What's new in 10.1.5

  • A new Non-Steam page spots any game running on your PC that is not from Steam, finds pictures for it and shows a temporary game page. Games from Rockstar, Epic, GOG and other launchers say Open launcher instead of Play.
  • Add games from outside Steam, with play time tracked, and an in-game overlay with your play time, the clock, your streak and new messages.
  • Themes are downloaded from GitHub with a screenshot of each one, collections are back, and there is an offline mode in Settings.
  • Messages: voice messages, group chats and GIFs. The streak celebration is back, and updates download inside SteamLite with a progress bar.
  • Profiles: switch between Steam accounts, or add another one, from your picture. Games with an anti-cheat are tracked correctly.
  • New engine: Tauri 2 instead of Electron, about 25 MB installer instead of about 103 MB.
  • Brand new interface with spring animations and pages that scroll up and down.
  • Messages: chat with friends, share photos and GIFs, mute, block and unblock, with notifications.
  • A News page and update notices you can open from anywhere.
  • Profile customisation: banner, tagline, title, avatar frame, accent and showcase.
  • Works with the SteamLite Updater and installs over your existing copy.

Drops, the shop, the trophy room, events, wishlist and sales, inventory and the tools hub are not in 10.1.5 yet and will return in the new design.
