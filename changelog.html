26.11

StableNovember 14, 2026

The first stable release of the new SteamLite.

  • A calm new look with plain covers and one green Play button on every game.
  • A clear library. Installed or not, play time and last played on every game.
  • Sign in with Steam, so SteamLite never sees your password.
  • A modern installer that also updates SteamLite in place.
  • Stable and Beta channels.
