---
title: SteamLite 9.2.0 Released - Sign in with Steam
date: 2026-10-07T11:46:00Z
tag: Release
---

SteamLite now asks you to sign in with Steam when you open it. After one quick restart it loads your games and profile, backs up your progress to the cloud, restores it on any PC and gives verified players a tick on the leaderboard. Steam confirms who you are, SteamLite never sees your password, and it keeps working offline once you are signed in.
