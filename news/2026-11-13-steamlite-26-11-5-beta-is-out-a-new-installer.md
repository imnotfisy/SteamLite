---
title: SteamLite 26.11.5.beta is out - a new installer
date: 2026-11-13T12:00:00Z
tag: Release
---

The sixth beta of the new SteamLite is out. The installer is now a modern animated window, and it is also how SteamLite updates: when an update is found, Update now downloads it, closes SteamLite and opens the installer, which updates SteamLite where it is and opens it again. Your games, collections and play time are kept. If you run an older installer, it tells you when a newer version is out and can get it for you. Game pictures also load more often, because SteamLite now tries more Steam image servers before giving up. Get it from the SteamLite page on GitHub (Releases, the 26.11.5.beta pre-release), or in the app if you use the beta update channel.
