---
title: SteamLite 10.0.3 Released - Profiles and an anti-cheat fix
date: 2026-10-21T11:39:00Z
tag: Release
---

SteamLite 10.0.3 is out. You can now switch between Steam accounts, or add another one, from your picture in the title bar, and a new profile opens on a setup page for its messages and notifications. Games with an anti-cheat are no longer counted as closed when their launcher quits.
