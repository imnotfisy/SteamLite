The new SteamLite is here as a beta for you to try. It has a new look, a faster and simpler app, and versions by year and month. Your library, accounts, favorites, collections and play time carry over. Messages, themes, the overlay and the old rewards system are gone, so SteamLite can focus on your games. Get it from the SteamLite page on GitHub (Releases, the 26.11.beta pre-release). If you use the beta update channel it will be offered to you in the app. It is a beta, so tell us what to fix.
