---
title: SteamLite 9.2.4 Released - Photos, voice messages and more
date: 2026-10-08T12:00:00Z
tag: Release
---

Messages now supports photos and GIFs (pick, paste or drop), voice messages, link previews, quick replies and pinned chats, and a What should we play? button that finds games everyone in the chat owns. It also works with the new SteamLite Mobile app, so friends on their phones can send and see the same things. Look out for the card in Messages that links to the app.
