SteamLite 26.11.8 is out. It is a small update about Discord: you can connect your Discord account, see a live preview of your Rich Presence card, and join the SteamLite community server from inside the app.

## What is new

- **Connect Discord.** In Settings, under Discord, press **Connect Discord** to link the Discord account that is signed in on your PC. The Discord app has to be open. SteamLite only reads which account it is, and nothing is sent anywhere. Your Discord name and picture show on your Profile page.
- **A preview of your Discord card.** Under the Discord switches, a preview shows how your profile will look, drawn like Discord draws it: the game and its art, the live timer, the rotating numbers and the buttons. It follows the switches before you save, and you can flip between **Playing a game** and **Browsing**.
- **Join the SteamLite Discord.** A **Join** button at the bottom of the Discord section opens the community server, where you can ask for help, share an idea or say hello.

## Update

SteamLite offers it by itself. You can also press **Check now** in Settings, under Updates.
