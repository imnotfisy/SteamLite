---
title: A new SteamLite is on the way
date: 2026-11-12T12:00:00Z
tag: Announcement
---

We are rebuilding SteamLite from the ground up: a new modern look, a faster and simpler app, and a new version numbering by year and month (the first release will be 26.11). Your library, profiles, collections and play time carry over. To keep things focused, Messages, themes and the in-game overlay will not return. Details will follow here.
