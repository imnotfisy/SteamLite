---
title: SteamLite 10.0.0 Released - A new engine and a new interface
date: 2026-10-21T11:18:00Z
tag: Release
---

SteamLite 10.0.0 is out. It runs on a new, lighter engine with a completely rebuilt interface, and Messages are back with notifications, photos and GIFs, and block and unblock. Drops, the shop, the trophy room, events, wishlist and sales, inventory and the tools hub are not in this version yet and will return in the new design.
