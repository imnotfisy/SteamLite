SteamLite 26.11.6 is out. It is a small update about how SteamLite moves: the animations are lighter and smoother, and the mouse wheel now scrolls smoothly.

## What is new

- **Optimised animations.** Pages and buttons move more smoothly and use less of your PC.
- **Tabs you can grab and drag.** The page follows your pointer as you drag between tabs.
- **A Play, Stop and Cancel button that changes shape.** It morphs between its states instead of jumping.
- **Lighter animations while a game is running.** Looping animations ease off while you play, so SteamLite stays out of the way.
- **Smooth scrolling.** The mouse wheel glides instead of jumping.

## Update

SteamLite offers it by itself. You can also press **Check now** in Settings, under Updates.
