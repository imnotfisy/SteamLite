The seventh beta is out, and it is the start of the line that gets new features before the stable release. It has the new base look, a clearer library with installed or not, play time and last played on every game, plain messages for empty and error states, and the Stable channel unlocked in Settings. Stay on the Beta channel to test new features first, or switch to Stable any time.
