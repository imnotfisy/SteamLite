---
title: SteamLite 26.11.4 is out - themes, and a GTA 6 theme until Dec 1
date: 2026-11-15T11:53:00Z
tag: Release
release: v26.11.4
---

Settings has a new Themes section with three looks: the Default look, the Original purple and blue look, and a GTA 6 theme. Pick one to see it straight away, then press Save. To celebrate the upcoming release of GTA 6, the GTA 6 theme can be picked from Nov 15 to Dec 1; after that it stays in the list, greyed out. This update also shows what changed the first time SteamLite opens after an update (you can turn that off in Settings), adds a Copy diagnostics button for bug reports, and lets you press / or Ctrl+F to jump to the library search. Update from inside SteamLite, or get the installer from the latest release on GitHub.
