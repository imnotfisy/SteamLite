---
title: SteamLite 9.2.3 Released - Account page, verified players and a richer Messages
date: 2026-10-08T11:53:00Z
tag: Release
---

Connect your Steam library once and every PC connects by itself, and Settings > Account is rebuilt. Messages got a big upgrade: an emoji picker, reactions, replies, edits, pins and search. You can share a game with a Buy on Steam button or a whole game list, see who is online and what they are playing, open profiles, and start friend challenges. Trusted players can carry a blue verified tick.
