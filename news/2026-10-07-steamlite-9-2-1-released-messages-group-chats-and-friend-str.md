---
title: SteamLite 9.2.1 Released - Messages, group chats and friend streaks
date: 2026-10-07T11:53:00Z
tag: Release
---

You can now message your friends inside SteamLite. Open Messages from the folder button in the header: add friends with a code or find your Steam friends who use SteamLite, chat privately or in group chats of up to 20, and keep a friend streak going by messaging each other every day. You can block and report anyone, and delete your own messages.
