title: SteamLite 9.4.0 Released - Control your PC from your phone
date: 2026-10-10T11:53:00Z

SteamLite Mobile can now show what you are playing on your PC and, if you turn on the new switch in Settings, Privacy, lock it, put it to sleep or close the game. Pairs with SteamLite Mobile 1.4.0.
