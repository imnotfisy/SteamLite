SteamLite 26.11.9 is out. It has a new Themes section, a Discord theme, Discord branding, a smoother switch, and a notice that the GTA 6 theme is ending early.

## The GTA 6 theme ends on 27 November

The GTA 6 theme was due to run until December 1. It now ends early: **it will not be available after 27 November 2026.** SteamLite shows a notice about this every time it opens until then. After that the theme is locked, and if you were using it SteamLite goes back to the default look. Update to 26.11.9 to see the notice.

## What is new

- **A reworked Themes section.** Each theme is now a small picture of the app in its own colours, so you can see what you are picking before you pick it. They are grouped into Always available and Limited time, and the one in use says so.
- **A Discord theme.** Charcoal and Discord blurple, with tighter corners and Discord green for Play. It is always available.
- **Discord branding.** The Discord section in Settings shows the Discord logo, and Connect Discord and Join are in Discord blurple with the Discord symbol. Your connected Discord name on the Profile page shows the symbol too.
- **A smoother switch.** Clicking a switch in Settings no longer freezes the knob for a moment before it jumps across. The knob now slides straight away with a small spring, stretches while you hold it, and the coloured track fades in instead of snapping.

## Update

SteamLite offers it by itself. You can also press **Check now** in Settings, under Updates.
