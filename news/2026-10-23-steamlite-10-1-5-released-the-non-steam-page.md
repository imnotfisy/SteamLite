title: SteamLite 10.1.5 Released - The Non-Steam page
date: 2026-10-23T12:00:00Z
tag: Release
---

SteamLite 10.1.5 is out. A new Non-Steam page spots any game running on your PC that is not from Steam and finds pictures for it, with a temporary game page you can add to your library. Games from Rockstar, Epic, GOG and other launchers now say Open launcher instead of Play.
