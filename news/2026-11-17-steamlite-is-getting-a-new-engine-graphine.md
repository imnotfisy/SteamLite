---
title: "SteamLite is getting a new engine: Graphine"
date: 2026-11-17T12:00:00Z
tag: Announcement
---

The team behind SteamLite are happy to announce that SteamLite will be getting a new engine. We changed from Electron to Rust back in 10.0.0, but now? 27.1 will involve our new engine we are working on: Graphine (100% SteamLite exclusive). No other app will use this engine (unless we give out permission). We will make the engine open source in 2028, once we have verified it's optimised and ready for other apps or games. 27.1 beta releases on the 15th of December. Until then, enjoy SteamLite, because it's about to get. Better.
