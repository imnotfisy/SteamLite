title: SteamLite 9.0.0 Released - Glass, Couch mode and more
date: 2026-10-04T12:00:00Z
tag: Release
---

SteamLite 9.0.0 is here. A new floating glass interface (Classic is one switch away), a full-page Settings, couch mode for your sofa with a page for every game, Spotify now playing in the top bar, a much better Discord presence card and a redesigned Tools & extras. It uses less memory and no idle CPU, and the Updater can now switch between Stable and Beta.
