A small update to the stable release. In News, you can now click any card. It grows into a full view with the whole post, the full notes of that release, and its pictures. Click a picture to see it full size, and press Esc or click outside to close. The beta, 26.11.8.beta, has the same change. Update from inside SteamLite, or get the installer from the latest release on GitHub.
