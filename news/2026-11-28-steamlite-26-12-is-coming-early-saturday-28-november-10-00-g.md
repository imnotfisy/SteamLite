The SteamLite team has finished development of SteamLite 26.12, so it is releasing early. It was planned for December 1, and it is now coming on **Saturday 28 November 2026 at 10:00 GMT**.

SteamLite offers the update by itself when it is out. You can also press **Check now** in Settings, under Updates.

## What is in it

SteamLite 26.12 adds the **Customise** page and modules, so you can make your own look and share it, a new **Account** page, **Sign in with Discord**, a library that stays quick with any number of games, and an optional preview of the next interface. The full list is in the release notes when it is out.

The Christmas theme still unlocks on December 1, with snow you can turn off.
