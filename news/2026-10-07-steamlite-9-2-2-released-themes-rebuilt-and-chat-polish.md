---
title: SteamLite 9.2.2 Released - Themes rebuilt and chat polish
date: 2026-10-07T12:00:00Z
tag: Release
---

Themes have a brand new window with live previews of every theme, a Community tab to find and download themes other players made, and a My library tab for yours. The new Theme Maker has presets, a live preview and a Publish button that shares your theme with everyone. Chat also got a big polish: typing indicators with the three-dot bubble, animated sending and receiving, message grouping and a new-messages pill.
