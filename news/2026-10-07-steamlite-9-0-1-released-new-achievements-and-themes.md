title: SteamLite 9.0.1 Released - New achievements and themes
date: 2026-10-07T11:11:00Z
tag: Release
---

SteamLite 9.0.1 is out. A tidier header with the extras in one folder, a floating now-playing pill you can move anywhere, 15 new achievements, 3 new themes (Royal Gold, Mocha Cream, Sakura Night), icons instead of emojis and a redesigned news widget. A level now takes 80,000 XP; your current level is unchanged.
