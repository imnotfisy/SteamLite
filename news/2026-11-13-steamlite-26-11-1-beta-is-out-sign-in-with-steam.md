---
title: SteamLite 26.11.1.beta is out - Sign in with Steam
date: 2026-11-13T11:53:00Z
tag: Release
---

The second beta of the new SteamLite is out. SteamLite now signs you in with Steam's own sign-in page, so typing a Steam ID and API key is gone. Everyone signs in with Steam once, even if you were already signed in, and your library, favorites, collections and play time are kept. It also adds an update channel picker in Settings, better game tracking (games that start through a launcher, and Steam games started from Steam), an animated Play and Stop button, and a few fixes. Get it from the SteamLite page on GitHub (Releases, the 26.11.1.beta pre-release), or in the app if you use the beta update channel.
